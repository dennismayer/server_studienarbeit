# Glossary

**Professor** — A registered user of the portal who offers Sprechstunden.

**Akademischer Titel (Academic title)** — The academic title a Professor carries: one of "Prof. Dr.", "Prof.", "Dr.", or none ("Kein Titel"). Forms of address such as "Herr"/"Frau" are deliberately not titles. Not to be confused with a Slot's title.

**Raum (Room)** — A bookable location from the fixed, centrally maintained list of rooms. A Slot's room is either one of these or none ("Kein Raum").

**Standardraum (Default room)** — The Room a Professor usually holds Sprechstunden in. Optional ("Kein Standardraum"). Used only as the suggested room when creating a new Slot.

**Slot (Sprechstunde / time slot)** — A one-time or weekly-recurring block of office-hour time on a given date. Its *title* is a free-text label for the appointment (e.g. "Offene Sprechstunde"), unrelated to the Akademischer Titel.
